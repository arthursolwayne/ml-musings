# Working memory total bits

## Calculation

4 chunks × bits per chunk

## Estimates

4 chunks × 1 bit = 4 bits (lower bound, binary)
4 chunks × 10 bits = 40 bits (typical, words)
4 chunks × 30 bits = 120 bits (upper bound, dense expert chunks)

## Result

~40-120 bits depending on expertise and encoding.
